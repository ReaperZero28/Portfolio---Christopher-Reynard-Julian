Project Overview

Tangsel Foodie Quiz (2026)

Foodie Quiz is a simple quiz game about food spots around South Tangerang. The game challenges users to test their knowledge of local food places while making it fun to explore and learn about food spots around South Tangerang.

Role

UI/UX Designer

Platform

Desktop, Mobile

Tool

Figma

Case Study

Problem

The client wanted Foodie Quiz to be more than just an entertaining game. It needed to be easy to play while encouraging users to test their knowledge of food and dining spots around South Tangerang. The game also needed a playful visual style inspired by the client’s existing map-style Instagram content featuring food spots around Gading Serpong, with friendly colors and hand-drawn illustrations to create a familiar and engaging experience.

The Map

What did I do?

Since the game is quite simple with a straightforward user flow, my process was relatively focused. I started by understanding the flow on how the game would work from start to finish. Once I had a clear understanding, I moved directly into wireframing to define the layout and content structure before developing the final high-fidelity designs.

Solution

I designed Foodie Quiz with a simple and straightforward game flow, making it easy for users to understand and play without unnecessary steps. The client clearly requested a playful visual style similar to their existing map, so I reused its color palette and hand-drawn food illustrations to maintain a familiar and consistent visual experience.
