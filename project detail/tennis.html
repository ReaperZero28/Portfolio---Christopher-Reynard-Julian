Project Overview

Tennis Scoreboard (Deuce) (2026)

Deuce is a tennis and padel scoring application designed to make it easier for users to manage scores during live matches. The application allows users to update scores in real time while also keeping a record of completed matches for future reference.

Role

UI/UX Designer

Platform

Desktop, Mobile

Tool

Figma

Case Study

Problem

During the development of Deuce, my team and I analyzed several tennis scoring applications available on the App Store to understand their existing features and user experience. We found that some scoring applications did not allow users to modify the score in the middle of an ongoing set, making it difficult to correct mistakes or adjust the score without restarting the scoring process from the beginning. This inspired us to create a more flexible scoring experience that would allow users to make changes throughout the match without having to start over.

What did I do?
  • Research & Analysis : I analyzed existing tennis scoring applications available on the App Store together with my team to understand their features, user flows, and potential pain points. This research helped us identify opportunities to create a more flexible scoring experience.
  • Requirement Gathering: Based on our research findings, I discussed and gathered requirements with the team to define the system flow and determine how the identified user problems could be addressed. We also explored and proposed new features that were not commonly available in other scoring applications.
  • Branding & Design System: As the UI Designer, I established the visual foundation of the application by defining the color palette, logo, typography, and other UI elements. The goal was to create a distinctive visual identity that could differentiate Deuce from existing tennis scoring applications.
  • High-fidelity Design After defining the requirements and system flow, I translated them into high-fidelity designs, focusing on creating a clear and easy-to-use interface while ensuring the proposed features could be accessed and understood naturally by users.
Solution

I designed the scoring experience to give users more flexibility when managing an ongoing match. On the scoring page, users can apply the score settings for each set and game, allowing them to correct or modify scores without having to restart the match from the beginning. I also designed the navigation for these settings to make them easy to access without interrupting the scoring flow. In addition to the core scoring experience, we introduced background customization and sponsor attachment features, allowing users to personalize the application background and add their own sponsor logos.
