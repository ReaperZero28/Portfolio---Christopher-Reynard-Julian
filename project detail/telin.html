Project Overview

Telin Commercial Event Webpage (2026)

Telin Commercial Event Webpage is a new landing page for Telin’s Commercial Event platform. The website showcases various commercial events while also providing access to other information and features, including Telin products, sales leaderboards, Kudos, and other related content.

Role

UI/UX Designer

Platform

Desktop, Mobile

Tool

Figma

Commercial Event Landing Page

Problem

The website was a redesign of an existing landing page that had previously been developed with an established visual style. The client wanted the new design to feel more creative and engaging while still maintaining consistency with the existing website. This meant exploring a new layout and presentation without losing the established color palette and UI elements that were already part of the previous landing page.

What did I do?
  • Design Analysis: I started by analyzing the previous landing page to understand its existing design style, layout, UI elements, and overall structure, since I was not involved in designing the previous version.
  • Requirements Gathering : I gathered the requirements for the new landing page and documented the content sections and menus that needed to be included, ensuring that all required information was covered in the new design.
  • Asset Collection : I collected and reviewed reusable elements from the previous landing page, including the color palette, image assets, buttons, cards, and typography, to maintain consistency with the existing design since it also helps me to save time in defining the UI foundation.
  • Wireframing: I created wireframes to explore the layout and content structure of the new landing page, making sure the different menus and sections were organized clearly and that users could get an overview of the available content.
  • High-fidelity Design Finally, after establishing the structure, I converted the wireframes into high-fidelity designs by introducing a more creative and engaging layout while maintaining the existing design elements and overall identity.
Solution

I redesigned the landing page by introducing a more creative and engaging layout that is quite different from the previous design. Then, I also reorganized the content and menus into a clearer structure so users could easily understand the different sections and access the information available on the platform. Existing elements such as buttons, cards, typography, and image assets were reused and adapted within the new layout to maintain consistency with Telin’s established identity while giving the landing page a refreshed experience.

Final Design

Gamification

Problem

Gamification is one of the features included in the new Telin Commercial Event landing page. The feature is designed to encourage Telin users to participate in various activities by allowing them to earn points, achieve different ranks, collect badges, and compete on a gamification leaderboard. Since the feature is built around gamification, the client wanted the experience to feel more like a game rather than a standard dashboard. The design needed to clearly communicate the user's progress and achievements through elements such as rank progression, monthly objectives, earned badges, and the gamification leaderboard, while still fitting within the overall design of the Telin Commercial Event landing page.

What did I do?
  • Requirement Analysis: I analyzed the requirements for the Gamification feature to understand how the scoring system, rank progression, monthly objectives, badges, and leaderboard would work within the overall experience.
  • Gamification Research : I explored references from various gamification and game interfaces to understand how progress, achievements, objectives, and rankings could be presented in an engaging and easy-to-understand way.
  • Visual Exploration : I explored different design approaches that could give the feature a more game-like feel while still maintaining the existing Telin design elements and overall identity of the Commercial Event landing page.
  • Wireframing: I created wireframes to establish the layout and structure of the Gamification page, including the placement of rank progress, monthly objectives, badges, and leaderboard.
  • High-fidelity Design I changed the wireframes into high-fidelity designs by incorporating game-inspired UI elements and visual treatments to make the feature feel more engaging while keeping the information clear and accessible.
Solution

I designed the Gamification feature with a game-inspired approach by incorporating clear progress and achievement elements throughout the page. Users can track their current rank and progress toward the next rank through a dedicated progress bar, while their monthly objectives are presented as a list to make their goals easy to follow. I also designed the profile card to showcase the badges users have earned, giving them a clear overview of their achievements. Lastly, I incorporated a podium-style leaderboard that allows users to see their ranking and compare their progress with other users. All of these elements were combined into a structured layout that creates a more engaging, game-like experience while remaining consistent with the overall Telin Commercial Event design.

Telin Kudos

Problem

Telin Kudos is designed to encourage engagement and communication between users by allowing them to give appreciation through emojis and respond to the Kudos they receive. Since the feature focuses on user interaction, the design needed to make each interaction easy to understand while maintaining a playful feel. It also needed to visually distinguish between Kudos that users send and receive without making the experience feel like a conventional messaging interface.

What did I do?

I explored different ways to present the Kudos interactions while keeping the experience easy to understand and engaging. I focused on how each interaction could be visually separated and how the sent and received Kudos could be distinguished clearly. I also explored different UI treatments and color combinations to create a playful feel that would suit the nature of the feature while still fitting within the overall Telin Commercial Event design.

Solution

I designed each Kudos interaction as an individual card to clearly distinguish between the Kudos that users send and receive. This card-based approach also helps the feature feel more like an appreciation platform rather than a conventional messaging interface. To make the interactions more engaging, I also implemented three main colors throughout the design, creating a more playful and visually varied experience while keeping the information easy to read.
