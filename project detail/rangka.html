Project Overview

Rangka.in Landing Page (2025)

Rangka.in is a physical therapy and Pilates studio that provides professional physiotherapy and Pilates services. The landing page introduces Rangka.in’s services, benefits, and wellness approach while providing visitors with the information they need to learn more about the studio and get started with their wellness journey.

Role

UI/UX Designer

Platform

Desktop, Mobile

Tool

Figma

Case Study

Problem

The client wanted the Rangka.in website to provide a user-friendly experience while maintaining the studio’s friendly and approachable brand identity. The website also needed to incorporate interactive UI elements with visual effects, such as pushed and fade effects, to make the overall experience more engaging without making the design feel overly complex.

What did I do?

I started by reviewing the requirements provided by the client through a detailed presentation, which clearly outlined the content, sections, and overall structure of the website. I then developed a color palette that aligned with Rangka.in’s branding while exploring the visual direction for the website, followed by collecting the necessary images and other visual assets. Since the requirements and structure were already clearly defined, I skipped the wireframing stage and moved directly into creating the high-fidelity design.

Solution

I designed this website with a simple, structured layout in every section, whilst maintaining a neat and easy-to-understand visual appearance without adding unnecessary elements. The design utilises Rangka.in’s signature green colour, generous spacing, circular cards, subtle shadows and approachable illustrations to create a welcoming health experience. I have also added interactive user interface (UI) components with effects such as pushed and fade animations to enhance user engagement, while keeping the overall experience simple and user-friendly.
