Project Overview

Pandora Experience Management Dashboard (Pandit) (2025 - 2026)

Pandit is a Pandora Experience management dashboard designed to centralize various operational activities, allowing internal staff to manage and monitor their work through a more structured and intuitive interface.

Role

UI/UX Designer

Platform

Desktop, Mobile

Tool

Figma

Pandit Dashboard

Problem

Pandit Dashboard is used by admins to manage various operational activities across Pandora Experience, including game room management, booking, financial operations, business process tracking, actor performance management, and more. With each activity involving different types of information and workflows, admins need a structured and intuitive system that makes it easier for them to access, organize, and monitor their day-to-day operations.

What did I do?
  • Research and Analysis: As this was my first project at the company, I started by understanding the existing user flow and how the system was used while also reviewing the existing mockups to understand the design elements and identify areas for improvement.
  • Requirements Gathering : After analyzing the existing flow, I gathered and redefined the requirements from the ground up to regain a clearer understanding of user needs and the improvements needed in the new flow
  • Wireframing: I created simple wireframes to help me in mapping out the new user flow before developing the high-fidelity mockup.
  • High-fidelity Design From the wireframes, I started to develop the final mockup design that is more engaging and intuitive while staying consistent with the brand guidelines.
Solution

I continued to use and follow the existing UI elements and design patterns to maintain consistency with the established brand guidelines, which helped me design and improve Pandit Dashboard into a more structured and intuitive platform for admins to access information, manage tasks, and monitor ongoing operations.

Pandit Web App

Problem

Pandit Web App is used by various internal staff beyond admins, including cashiers, construction technicians, creative staff, and actors, each with different responsibilities and day-to-day tasks. With these different roles and responsibilities, staff need an app that is easy to access and use, allowing them to efficiently perform their specific tasks and manage their daily activities.

What did I do?

Similar to Pandit Dashboard, I followed the same design process for Pandit Web App since the app was already in place, starting by understanding the existing user flow and continuing through to creating high-fidelity designs that addressed user needs while maintaining the same existing UI elements and design patterns.

Solution

I designed Pandit Web App as a role-based platform that provides each staff member with the tools and features relevant to their responsibilities. The app brings different workflows into dedicated features, including customer refund processing for cashiers, to-do list management for construction technicians, actor performance evaluation for creative staff, and performance history for actors to review their own evaluations. This allows each role to focus on their specific tasks through a more seamless and intuitive experience while maintaining a consistent interface across the platform.

Pandora Experience Affiliator

Problem

Pandora Affiliate is designed to be used by two different parties: customers who become affiliates (Affiliator) and internal admins who manage the affiliate program. Affiliator needs a dashboard to track their own affiliate performance and progress, while admin needs to manage and monitor affiliate activities through the existing Pandit Dashboard. This requires the system to provide two different experiences that support the needs of each party within the same affiliate program.

What did I do?
  • Research & Analysis I started by researching how affiliate programs are implemented across other platforms, including Alfagift, Tokopedia, and Blibli, to understand their approaches, features, and user behaviors. This helped me gain insights into common affiliate workflows and identify opportunities for the Pandora Affiliate experience.
  • Requirements Gathering: I started by reviewing and refining the requirements based on discussions with the client to clearly define the needs of both affiliates and admins.
  • User Flow Mapping: I mapped out the user flows for both the affiliate and admin dashboards to define how each user would navigate and interact with the platform.
  • Design System Development: Since the affiliator dashboard is a new dashboard, I coducted research and developed the necessary UI elements, including colors, typography, icons, and illustration assets to create a consistent foundation for the interface.
  • Wireframing: I created wireframes focused on the layout and content structure of the affiliate dashboard, using the defined requirements to establish its visual direction and determine how information and features should be presented.
  • High-fidelity Design: Once the wireframes were finalized, I developed the high-fidelity designs for both dashboards. I applied the newly established design system to the affiliate dashboard, while following the existing design patterns from Pandit Dashboard for the Affiliate Admin Dashboard.
Solution

I designed Pandora Affiliate to provide two connected experiences for affiliates and admins. Affiliates have their own dedicated dashboard where they can track their affiliate performance and progress, while affiliate management is integrated into the Pandit Dashboard, allowing admins to manage and monitor affiliate activities within the existing system. This approach keeps both experiences focused on their respective needs while maintaining a consistent connection between the affiliate and admin sides of the platform.
