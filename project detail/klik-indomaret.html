Project Overview

Klik Indomaret Shopping Flow (2024)

Klik Indomaret is an online shopping application by Indomaret that allows users to purchase products without having to visit the store. Similar to other e-commerce platforms, the application provides a convenient shopping experience where users can browse products, place orders, and have their purchases delivered directly to their location.

Role

UI/UX Designer

Platform

Desktop, Mobile

Tool

Figma

Case Study

Problem

After analyzing the existing shopping flow in the Klik Indomaret application, I found several areas that could still be improved to make the overall experience more efficient and straightforward. I also noticed that some of the UI components used throughout the shopping process could be utilized more effectively. These observations led me to explore alternative approaches to the existing user flow and UI components to create a smoother shopping experience.

What did I do?
  • User Flow Analysis : I started by analyzing the existing shopping flow in the Klik Indomaret application to understand how users navigate through the process, from browsing products to completing their orders. This helped me identify areas where the flow could be simplified and improved.
  • Flow Exploration: Based on my analysis, I explored alternative user flows to find a more efficient way for users to complete their shopping journey. I focused on reducing unnecessary steps and making the overall process easier to understand.
  • UI Component Analysis: I also reviewed the existing UI components used throughout the shopping flow and identified several components that could be used more effectively. I explored alternative component treatments that could better support the user's tasks and interactions.
  • High-fidelity Design After establishing the improved flow and component approach, I translated the exploration into high-fidelity designs to demonstrate how the proposed changes could be applied to the application.
Solution

I redesigned the shopping flow by simplifying several steps and restructuring the navigation to make the overall process more straightforward. I also explored improvements to several UI components to make their purpose and interactions clearer, allowing users to complete their shopping tasks with less friction. The proposed design focuses on creating a more efficient shopping experience while maintaining the familiar e-commerce flow that users are already accustomed to.
