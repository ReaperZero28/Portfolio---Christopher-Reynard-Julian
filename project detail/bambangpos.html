Project Overview

Bambangpos.id Game App (2026)

Bambangpos.id is a game app developed by Komune Studio Software House, inspired by Wordle, where users are challenged to guess words based on the images provided. The game takes a lighthearted approach by incorporating “bapak-bapak” jokes, a type of humor commonly found in Indonesian culture, creating a casual and entertaining guessing experience.

Role

UI/UX Designer

Platform

Desktop, Mobile

Tool

Figma

Case Study

Problem

My team and I wanted to create a game inspired by Wordle while introducing a slightly different gameplay approach so that it would have its own identity. We also wanted the game to be playable and user-friendly, while incorporating the distinctive “bapak-bapak” humor into the overall experience. This meant finding a balance between creating a game that was easy to understand and play, while also making the interactive experience feel playful and relatable to Indonesian users.

What did I do?
  • Gameplay Analysis : I started by analyzing Wordle’s gameplay together with my team to understand its core mechanics, user flow, and overall experience. This helped us identify which aspects could be adapted and which areas could be changed to create a different gameplay experience.
  • Brainstorming: After analyzing the gameplay, my team and I held brainstorming sessions to explore different ideas and alternative gameplay for making Bambangpos.id distinct from Wordle.
  • Requirements Gathering: Once we established the gameplay concept, we defined the requirements needed for the game and determined the visual direction, including how the “bapak-bapak” humor could be represented through the overall look and feel of the game.
  • Wireframing: As the UI Designer, I created wireframes to establish the layout structure and organize the different elements needed throughout the gameplay experience.
  • Visual Exploration: I explored playful typography and searched for illustrations of relatable Indonesian “bapak-bapak” characters and everyday situations that could support the theme and personality of the game.
  • Mockup Design After establishing the structure and visual direction, I translated the wireframes into high-fidelity mockups, combining the playful visual style with the gameplay requirements we had defined.
Solution

To create a gameplay experience that felt different from Wordle, we decided to incorporate a Hangman-inspired mechanic into Bambangpos.id. Instead of showing whether a correctly guessed letter is in the wrong position, users have a limited number of lives and will lose a life whenever they guess a wrong letter. We also introduced a point-based system where each completed guess is given a score, allowing users to track their performance. In addition, users can access a gallery containing their previous guesses along with the points they earned, giving them a way to look back at their previous attempts.
