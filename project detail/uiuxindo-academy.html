Project Overview

UIUXINDO Academy Revamp (2026)

UIUXINDO Academy is an online learning platform by UIUXINDO, a digital media platform focused on UI/UX and product design. The platform provides learning programs to help people improve their digital product design skills while also creating opportunities to connect with others from different regions and discuss topics related to UI/UX and product design.

Role

UI/UX Designer

Platform

Desktop, Mobile

Tool

Figma

Case Study

Problem

The existing UIUXINDO Academy website had a visual design that was no longer up-to-date with current design trends. Therefore, as part of UIUXINDO, my team and I decided to explore a redesign of the platform to improve its overall visual experience and make the website feel more modern, engaging, and relevant to its audience.

What did I do?

I started by analyzing the existing UIUXINDO Academy website to understand its current visual style, layout, navigation, and overall user experience. I then explored different design references and visual directions to find a more modern approach that would suit the platform and its audience. I kept the existing font and color palette to maintain the website’s established visual identity, while exploring different layouts and visual components to give the platform a more modern look and feel. I also created wireframes to explore the layout and content structure before translating them into high-fidelity designs, refining the visual hierarchy and UI elements to create a more modern, engaging, and consistent experience.

Solution

I redesigned the UIUXINDO Academy website with a more modern and engaging approach while keeping its core purpose as a learning platform intact. I improved the hierarchy, layout structure, typography, colors, and UI components to create a cleaner and more up-to-date experience. The redesigned interface also organizes learning programs and other content more clearly, making it easier for users to explore available programs and navigate through the platform. Overall, the redesign aims to give UIUXINDO Academy a stronger and more modern digital presence that better represents its focus on UI/UX and product design.
