Project Overview

Read Indonesia Landing Page (2025)

Read Indonesia is a platform used by the Ministry of Culture as part of its efforts to promote Indonesian literature. The website showcases works by Indonesian authors and provides opportunities for the international publishing industry to secure funding to translate Indonesian works into other languages.

Role

UI/UX Designer

Platform

Desktop, Mobile

Tool

Figma

Case Study

Problem

As a platform dedicated to promoting Indonesian literature, Read Indonesia was expected to have a visual identity that could represent the richness of Indonesia literary works while also have a familiar feeling as an e-library platform. The website needed to balance these two aspects so that its literary character remained visible without compromising the usability and familiarity expected from an e-library experience.

What did I do?
  • Requirements Gathering : I started by analyzing the requirements based on the brand identity to define the content and goals of the website to avoid too much information.
  • Research and Analysis: I analyzed various e-library platforms to understand their common features, structures, user behavior, and potential pain points. This helped me identify familiar patterns and elements that users would expect to find in an e-library platform.
  • User Flow & Wireframing: Based on the requirements and analysis, I mapped out the user flows and created wireframes to establish the overall structure and interactions of the website. This helped me organize the different features and define how users would navigate through the platform.
  • Asset Collection: I collected book covers and other relevant visual assets featuring Indonesian literary works to support the content and visual direction of the platform.
  • High-fidelity Design I translated the established structure and visual direction into the final high-fidelity designs, combining the patterns of an e-library platform with visual elements that reflect Indonesian literature.
Solution

I designed Read Indonesia with a familiar e-library structure while incorporating a visual style inspired by Indonesian literature. The layout and features were based on common elements found across the e-library platforms I analyzed, making it easier for users to browse and explore the available works, while book imagery and typograph help establish the platform’s literary identity.
